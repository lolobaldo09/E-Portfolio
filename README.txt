# Field Study 1 & 2 E-Portfolio

## Files
- `index.html` — content and page sections
- `style.css` — colors, layout, cards, responsive design
- `script.js` — active navigation and expandable weekly/activity sections
- `images/` — place your approved classroom/school background image here

## Preview on your computer
1. Keep all files in the same folder.
2. Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser.
3. Edit the sample text in `index.html` and save to see your changes.

## Add a background image
1. Put your image inside the `images` folder.
2. Rename it `classroom-background.jpg`.
3. Refresh the website. Use only photos you have permission to publish.

## Publish with GitHub Pages
1. Create a **public** repository on GitHub.
2. Upload `index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, and the `images` folder.
3. Open repository **Settings → Pages**.
4. Under Build and deployment, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Choose branch `main` and folder `/(root)`, then click **Save**.
6. Wait a few minutes and open the published site link shown on the Pages screen.

Before publishing, replace sample text and remove personal information you do not want public. GitHub Pages is public website hosting; do not upload confidential school records, private student information, or documents without permission.


Section menu: A round Facebook-style three-line button is positioned at the upper-left. Click it to open the section list; select a section to jump there. Click outside or press Escape to close it.
